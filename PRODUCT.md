# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, agency leads and startup founders who need AI working inside the tools they already run (CRM, WhatsApp, accounting, internal apps). They arrive from Upwork, LinkedIn, Google or a referral, usually comparing a few freelancers, and want proof that this person ships production systems rather than demos.

## Product Purpose

varmiguemunoz.com is the personal site of Miguel Angel Muñoz, AI Integration Engineer. It exists to turn a qualified visitor into a discovery call or a short written brief through the contact form. Success means a booked call or a message describing a real problem.

## Positioning

One engineer who owns the whole stack around the AI: the agents, the backends (Go, Node.js) and the frontends, embedded in systems the client already uses. The proof is systems running in production on real company data, with measured results.

## Operating Context

Visitors read in English. Many are evaluating him next to an Upwork profile. Calls are booked through a Google Calendar appointment link; written briefs go through the site contact form. Miguel works in English and Spanish and is based in Medellín, Colombia.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site language is English only. No em dashes or en dashes in copy; no "·" separators in prose. Arrow glyphs on buttons and links are allowed.
- Rate shown publicly: $23 per hour, or fixed price for defined deliverables.
- Astro site deployed on Vercel. Canonical domain: https://www.varmiguemunoz.com. GrowthlyFast is a retired brand.
- Existing blog posts stay published, including the 2023 posts.

## Brand Commitments

Name shown as "varmiguemunoz" in the nav and "Miguel Angel Muñoz" as the person. Voice: direct, concrete, first person, short sentences, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- AI Finance Assistant on WhatsApp: 80% less manual finance work, 35+ hours saved per week.
- WhatsApp AI platform for sales and support: 1,000+ conversations a day in production, about 70% automated, 45+ hours saved per week. 1,000+ is the confirmed figure.
- AI lead sourcing and scoring pipeline (ZoomInfo, Claude, Pipedrive): 40+ hours saved per week.
- Automated lead generation system: 70,000+ monthly views.
- Also: Daylios, SprintOS (sprintos.run), Lanatecuenta, TaoFlow, AI tutor agent.
- 15+ AI systems in production, 10,000+ operations a day across clients.
- Client reviews from Upwork, Google and LinkedIn (src/components/ops/data.ts). Portrait at public/avatar.webp.
- Client names and screenshots of client systems are confidential and must not be shown or invented.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over promise: every claim maps to a real system or a real review.
2. Show the work, not AI decoration: no generic node-and-arrow diagrams.
3. One clear next step on every screen: book a call or write two lines.
